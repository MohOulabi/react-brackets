Headless navigation and zoom. `Bracket.Root` with `Bracket.Viewport`, `Bracket.GroupTitle`, `Bracket.Pagination` (`PageButton`, `PreviousPage`, `NextPage`, `PageIndicator`) and `Bracket.ZoomControls` (`ZoomIn`, `ZoomOut`, `ZoomLevel`, `ZoomReset`) arrange the bracket your own way; each part draws a default look without children, and takes children, a function of its state, or a style. `usePagination()` and `useZoom()` drive your own controls from anywhere inside the root, with the swipe position and zoom scale as shared values. New `zoom` options (limits, step, where it starts, alignment, pinch, pan, double tap, momentum, elasticity, overshoot, animation, `onZoomChange`) and `swipe` options (focus, commit, projection, elasticity, spring) replace the fixed values, with defaults that keep today's behaviour. `<Bracket>` gains `renderPagination` and `renderZoomControls`, and `labels` gains the strings for the round tabs and zoom buttons. The default zoom controls are a pill of zoom out, the zoom level and zoom in, with a round fit button beside it; each is disabled where it would do nothing.

Compose the parts yourself when `<Bracket>` isn't enough. Every part:

- renders a real element with `data-scope="bracket"` and its `data-part`,
- passes any other prop through to that element, like `onClick`, `style` or `aria-*`,
- takes `className` as a string, or as a function of its state.

```tsx
<Bracket.Root stage={stage}>
  <Bracket.GroupTitle />
  <Bracket.Viewport>
    <Bracket.Rounds>
      {() => (
        <Bracket.Round>
          <Bracket.RoundTitle />
          <Bracket.Matches>
            {() => (
              <Bracket.Match>
                <Bracket.Participant slot={0} />
                <Bracket.Participant slot={1} />
              </Bracket.Match>
            )}
          </Bracket.Matches>
        </Bracket.Round>
      )}
    </Bracket.Rounds>
    <Bracket.Connectors />
  </Bracket.Viewport>
  <Bracket.Pagination />
</Bracket.Root>
```

## Bracket.Root

Draws one group, or several on one grid. Everything inside reads from it.

## Bracket.Viewport

The window the rounds are drawn in: scrolling, scaled, zoomable, or paged on narrow containers.

## Bracket.Rounds

Calls its children once per column, left to right.

<ApiTable from="react" name="RoundsProps" />

## Bracket.Round

One column. Takes any prop of a `section`. `className` receives [`RoundState`](/docs/api/hooks-and-types#roundstate).

## Bracket.GroupTitle and Bracket.RoundTitle

An `h2` with the group's name and an `h3` with the round's name. Children replace the name.

## Bracket.Matches

The list of matches in a column. Its `children` renders one match:

```tsx
<Bracket.Matches>{(match) => <MyCard match={match} />}</Bracket.Matches>
```

## Bracket.Match

A match card. Connector lines attach to it, and it becomes a button when the root has `onMatchClick`.

<ApiTable from="react" name="MatchOwnProps" />

## Bracket.Participant

One side of a match. By default it shows the name and score.

<ApiTable from="react" name="ParticipantOwnProps" />

```tsx
<Bracket.Participant slot={0}>
  {({ participant, winner, slot }) => (
    <>
      <Flag code={participant?.meta?.country} />
      <span className={winner ? 'font-bold' : ''}>{participant?.name ?? 'TBD'}</span>
      <span>{slot.score}</span>
    </>
  )}
</Bracket.Participant>
```

## Bracket.Connectors

The lines between matches, as one SVG. Place it inside `Bracket.Viewport`. Its `children` renders one line:

```tsx
<Bracket.Connectors radius={8}>{() => <Bracket.Connector strokeDasharray="4 4" />}</Bracket.Connectors>
```

<ApiTable from="react" name="ConnectorsOwnProps" />

## Bracket.Connector

One line, with its `data-*` attributes and theme styling. Takes any prop of an SVG `path`, including your own `d`. `className` receives [`ConnectorState`](/docs/api/hooks-and-types#connectorstate). See [connector lines](/docs/customize/connectors) for recipes.

## Bracket.Pagination

Round navigation for the paged view: previous, a button per round, next. It renders nothing while the whole tree is shown. Give it children to build your own from the parts below or [`usePagination()`](/docs/api/hooks-and-types#usepagination). See [navigation and zoom](/docs/customize/navigation-and-zoom) for recipes.

<ApiTable from="react" name="PaginationProps" />

## Bracket.PageButton

A button that moves the paged view to one round, showing the round's name. `className` and children can be functions of `{ round, index, active }`.

<ApiTable from="react" name="PageButtonProps" />

## Bracket.PreviousPage and Bracket.NextPage

One round back, or one on, with an arrow. Disabled at the first and last round. `className` and children can be functions of `{ disabled }`.

<ApiTable from="react" name="StepButtonProps" />

## Bracket.PageIndicator

A mark that slides under the page button of the round being shown, and runs ahead with a swipe. Place it next to the `Bracket.PageButton`s it follows.

<ApiTable from="react" name="PageIndicatorProps" />

## Bracket.ZoomControls

The zoom controls of a `pan-zoom` viewport: zoom out, the level and zoom in, then fit. Works anywhere inside `Bracket.Root`, and renders nothing while there is no pan and zoom viewport. Give it children to build your own from the parts below or [`useZoom()`](/docs/api/hooks-and-types#usezoom).

<ApiTable from="react" name="ZoomControlsProps" />

## Bracket.ZoomIn, Bracket.ZoomOut and Bracket.ZoomReset

Zoom in or out by a step around the centre, or fit the whole tree back into view. Each is disabled where it can't act. `className` and children can be functions of `{ scale, percent, disabled }`.

<ApiTable from="react" name="ZoomButtonProps" />

The zoom as a percentage, announced to screen readers as it changes. Children can be a function of `{ scale, percent }`.

- Round one pairs the top half against the bottom half (1 v 5, 2 v 6…).
- Later rounds pair players on the same record, highest first, and never repeat a pairing.
- With an odd field, the lowest-ranked player without a bye sits out and gets a win.
- `rounds` defaults to `⌈log₂(players)⌉`, enough to find one unbeaten player.

`nextSwissRound` throws if the current round is unfinished or every round has been played, so your UI can't get out of step.

Every option, with its type and default, is in the [API reference](/docs/api/formats#swiss).

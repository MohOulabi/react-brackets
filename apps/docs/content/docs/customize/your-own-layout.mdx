The library has two layers:

- **The core** knows the tournament: who plays whom, who won, who moved on, and which match feeds which. It draws nothing.
- **The parts**, `<Bracket>` and `Bracket.*`, draw the core's data as cards in columns, with lines between them.

The parts can look like anything, but they always lay matches out in columns. For another shape, like the rings above, use the core and draw it yourself, with SVG, a canvas or anything else. Results, live scores and `reportResult` work the same, whatever draws them.

## How the rings work

Every round's slots say who stands where. The outer ring is the first round's slots, each ring further in is the next round's, and the champion is the final's winner:

```tsx
// `stage` is any single elimination stage, from singleElimination or your own data.
const rounds = stage.groups[0].rounds;
const final = rounds.at(-1).matches[0];

const rings = rounds.map((round) =>
  round.matches.flatMap((match) => match.slots.map((slot) => slot.participantId)),
);
rings.push([final.winner === null ? null : final.slots[final.winner].participantId]);
```

The rest is placing them:

- The first ring runs down the left side and then down the right, so the two halves meet at the final.
- Every spot further in sits halfway between the two spots it comes from, so a winner lines up with the match it won.
- A team that stands on one ring but not on the next one in is out, and is drawn faded.
- `null` is a spot still to be decided.

Hover a team in the demo to light its path: every spot with the same `participantId`. The whole demo is about 150 lines, in [`radial.tsx`](https://github.com/MohOulabi/react-brackets/blob/main/apps/docs/components/demos/radial.tsx).

## What you build yourself

Drawing your own shape means the parts' extras are yours to add when you want them: highlighting on hover, clicks, swiping between rounds and pan-zoom. Each is a few lines on top of the core's data. If your shape is still columns, restyle the parts instead: see [Match cards](/docs/customize/match-cards), [Connectors](/docs/customize/connectors) and [Building from parts](/docs/customize/building-from-parts).

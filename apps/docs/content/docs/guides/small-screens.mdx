title: Small screens
description: A swipeable bracket on phones, or the whole tree with scroll, fit, or pan and zoom.
---

Below `compactBelow` (640px by default) the bracket pages through one round at a time. The width that counts is the **container's**, not the window's, so a bracket in a sidebar or a modal adapts too.

## The paged bracket

<Preview>
  <PagedBracketDemo />
</Preview>

With `compact="bracket"` (the default), the round in focus stacks tightly and the next round peeks in with its connector lines. Swipe or tap a round and every match glides into place. Swiping past the first or last round meets a soft rubber-band resistance.

A long or fast swipe crosses several rounds at once, the way a native carousel does. Set `maxSwipeRounds={1}` to page one round at a time.

`compact="list"` shows each round as a plain list of matches instead.

```tsx
<Bracket stage={stage} />                       // swipeable bracket
<Bracket stage={stage} compact="list" />        // swipeable lists
<Bracket stage={stage} maxSwipeRounds={1} />    // one round per swipe
<Bracket stage={stage} compactBelow={480} />    // switch at a different width
```

### Space between rounds

Each round in the paged view gets a column about three quarters of the bracket's width, and `--rb-column-gap` (`1.5rem` here) keeps the next round away from it. The theme keeps every card within its column, so that gap and the connector lines in it hold on the narrowest screen. To change the space, set the variable rather than padding the rounds:

```css
[data-scope="bracket"][data-part="root"][data-view="paged"] {
  --rb-column-gap: 2rem;
}
```

Without the theme, give your cards `max-width: 100%` and put the gap on the `content` part with `column-gap`.

### Height between rounds

Rounds have different heights: the first round of eight matches is taller than the semifinals. By default (`pageHeight="contain"`), the bracket stays tall enough for both rounds while they move, and settles to the new round's height once the move lands, so a round is never cut off while it is on screen.

`pageHeight="follow"` blends the two heights under your finger instead. The page below moves with the swipe, but the taller round is cut as it slides.

```tsx
<Bracket stage={stage} pageHeight="follow" />
```

To keep the page below from moving at all, give the bracket's container a `min-height` of its tallest round.

## The whole tree, on any screen

Set `compactBelow={0}` to always show the full tree, then choose how it fits with `mode`:

<Preview className="grid gap-8 md:grid-cols-3">
  <FullTreeDemo mode="scroll" />
  <FullTreeDemo mode="fit" />
  <FullTreeDemo mode="pan-zoom" />
</Preview>

| `mode` | Behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| `'scroll'` (default) | Native horizontal scrolling. |
| `'fit'` | Scaled down to fit the width available. Best for previews and dashboards. |
| `'pan-zoom'` | Drag, pinch, Ctrl/⌘ + scroll or double click to zoom, with zoom controls. A flick glides on, and the edges give a little before springing back. |

```tsx
<Bracket stage={stage} compactBelow={0} mode="pan-zoom" />
```

Every pan and zoom behaviour, from the limits to the bounce at the edges, is an option of `zoom`, and the zoom controls are parts you can restyle, move or replace. See [navigation and zoom](/docs/customize/navigation-and-zoom).
